Manufacturer Background

A Reliable Production Partner for Wholesalers & Food Businesses

Jay Bajrang Foods was established by Sunil Gupta with a clear purpose: to provide commercial food vendors, snack repackagers, farsan marts, and wholesale dealers with fresh, consistently crispy, and pure savory products at authentic manufacturer rates.

In the savory snack business, your customers return only when the crunch and taste remain the exact same every single week. We ensure this by maintaining rigorous standards in our frying process:

  • Zero Compromise on Cooking Medium: We use fresh, high-smoke point food-grade edible oils to prevent any oiliness, rancid smell, or darkening of sev.
  • Fine Milling & Sieving: For our signature Nylon Sev, we use precision-sieved chana dal besan that passes through ultra-fine zero-number dies for a delicate, feather-light crunch.
  • Moisture-Controlled Roasting: Our Mumra (Puffed Rice) is thoroughly cleaned to remove dust and unpopped grains, then roasted to stay crisp even in humid coastal weather.
  • Direct Manufacturer Pricing: You deal directly with the factory owner Sunil Gupta, without brokers or middlemen taking a cut from your wholesale margins.
