# Jay Bajrang Foods - Official Website

Authentic, natural, and handcrafted static website for **Jay Bajrang Foods**, owned & founded by **Sunil Gupta** in **Mira Road, Mumbai, Maharashtra**.

---

## 🌾 Design Philosophy: Handcrafted & Authentic

This website was built to look like a genuine, grounded Indian food manufacturing business website — not a generic AI SaaS template:
1. **Warm, Authentic Indian Color Palette**:
   - Deep traditional vermillion red (`#8a1414` / `#9e1a1a`), derived from the official logo.
   - Warm mustard & roasted spice tones (`#92400e` / `#c27803`).
   - Clean, natural paper canvas background (`#faf7f2` and pure `#ffffff`).
2. **Traditional Typography**:
   - Classic serif headings (`Lora`) for a credible, established manufacturer feel.
   - Clean, legible sans-serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) for product specs and business details.
3. **Honest, Practical Content**:
   - Real wholesale details: gauge/cut (0-Number Nylon Sev), oil quality, pack sizes (5kg, 10kg, 20kg), and end-use applications (Sev Puri, Bhel, Poha topping, Farsan retail).
   - Clear transport routes across Western Line, Central Line, Harbour Line, and Maharashtra transport dispatch.
   - Personal statement from proprietor Sunil Gupta.

## 🚀 How to Run & Preview

1. **Directly in Browser:** Double-click [`index.html`](file:///Users/sachingupta/Desktop/sachin/jbf/jbfwebsite/index.html) to open in Chrome, Safari, or Edge.
2. **Local HTTP Server:**
   ```bash
   cd jbfwebsite
   python3 -m http.server 8000
   ```
   Then visit `http://localhost:8000`.
